    Security Deposit, Venue Inspection & Safety Regulations

    Contractor must comply with all listed rules

    Security Deposit

    The Contractor/Vendor shall deposit AED 10,000 as a security deposit upon signing the Work Permit. It protects against venue structure/equipment damage and is refundable within seven business days after the event ends, provided no damages occur.

    Venue Inspection

    The Contractor shall inspect the event venue prior to commencing setup and report any unsatisfactory conditions in writing to Property Management. Failure to report makes the Contractor responsible for the venue condition.

    Safety Regulations Must Be Followed

    • Valid photo ID is required to gain access from P1 security office.
    • All staff must enter and exit via the Security Entrance.
    • All personnel must have valid, job-specific experience.
    • Signed Work Permit must be brought to the premises as proof of access.
    • Required PPE must be worn.
    • Contractors must be reasonably dressed and meet property hygiene standards.
    • Certified helmet and harness must be worn above 1.8 meters.
    • Portable electrical equipment must use grounded approved plugs and sockets.
    • No work on live electrical distribution boards; power isolation must follow hotel lockout/tagout policy.
    • Cables must pass through cable ramps and avoid doorways.
    • Electrical loads must be within supply capacity and tested with the property engineer.
    • Marble floor and carpets must be protected during setup and breakdown.
    • Smoke/Haze machines are prohibited unless approved 48 hours prior.
    • Flammable materials must be fire-retardant treated with certificates submitted.
    • No excessively loud construction work unless permitted by Property Management.
    • Equipment must have up-to-date test certificates.
    • Contractors are responsible for their own equipment and tools.
    • No attachment to building fabric except designated venue hanging points.
    • Safe and environmentally friendly practices must be followed.
    • Smoking and alcohol consumption are forbidden except in designated areas.
    • Unsafe work may be suspended until corrected at contractor cost.
    • Work areas must be secured to protect guests and prevent unauthorized access.
    • Work areas must be clean and tidy.
    • Contractors and staff may not access public areas without written permission.
    • Emergency exit doors and escape routes must never be blocked or obstructed for any reason.
    • Proper protection is required if activities may damage hotel property.
    • Candles require Property Management approval in accordance with safety standards.
    • Loud or abusive language will not be tolerated.
    • After setup, garbage must be cleared off-site and the venue returned as received.
    • Non-compliance may result in work cessation, permit cancellation, cleaning fees, repair costs, and waste removal charges.

    Legal Declaration, Agreement & Acceptance

    • I/We understand the obligations under the Property OSHAD Standards that are applicable to the work being undertaken and to the circumstances in which the contract will be performed.
    • I/We confirm that we have received a copy of, read, and understood the Contractors Induction training requirements, and will comply with the hotel safety rules and Contractor Management Procedure.
    • I/We confirm that the contractor, subcontractor, authorised signatory, supervisors, and personnel hold all certifications, licenses, competencies, and qualifications required by applicable legislation, regulations, and the work scope.
    • I/We will immediately cease work, make the workplace safe, and contact the Property Event Manager if I/we become aware of any danger to myself/ourselves, others, guests, staff, contractors, subcontractors, the venue, or property assets during the contract period.
    • I/We acknowledge and agree that any injury, illness, death, loss, or damage involving contractors, subcontractors, personnel, equipment, or the venue arising from contractor/subcontractor acts, omissions, defects, negligence, unsafe work practices, or non-compliance shall remain the full responsibility and liability of the contractor/subcontractor. The management of the Property shall not be liable for accidents, injuries, death, losses, or damages involving contractors or subcontractors to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.
    • I/We agree that, in the event of any dispute between the Property and the contractor/subcontractor, all necessary procedures shall be taken in accordance with the laws of the United Arab Emirates, with jurisdiction before the competent courts of Abu Dhabi, UAE.
